    Settings

    Compact method

    Double-click compact to open the full calendar. Double-click the calendar to enter compact.

    Show / hide hotkey

    Click the box, then press a shortcut. Opens or hides the gadget anywhere.

    Display time zone

    Sorted by current local time (UTC offset). Used for the clock and alarm target times.

    Help

    — Window —

    • Show / hide — tray click or Ctrl+Alt+C
    • Right-click calendar — show toolbar
    • Drag clock area — move window
    • Toolbar × — hide gadget
    • Ctrl+Shift+P — pin / unpin always on top

    — Compact clock —

    • Settings → Compact method: method1 or method2
    • method1 — small time chip at right edge
    • method2 — larger compact clock
    • Double-click compact — open full calendar
    • Double-click calendar — enter compact
    • Minimize button — toggle compact / expand
    • Pinned + compact — clicks pass through until you hover

    — Calendar —

    • ‹ › or mouse wheel — change month
    • Today — jump to current day
    • Click a day — meetings for that date

    — Timer / alarm —

    • Timer — set target time (scroll wheels)
    • Countdown & alerts while a target is set
    • Hour marks & 30-min alert before target
    • Alarm at target — dismiss from panel

    — Settings —

    • Opacity, week start, time zone
    • Show / hide hotkey (customizable)
    • Window size limits

    — Tray —

    • Icon shows today’s date number
    • Click tray icon — show full calendar
